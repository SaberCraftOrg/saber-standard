# Live Classes

SaberCraft, the founding school of the Saber Standard, runs free in-person saber classes in Miami. No experience or equipment required — walk in, borrow a saber, and train.

<div class="admonition note" markdown>
<p class="admonition-title">What a class covers</p>
<p>A single Fundamentals session (ages 17+) runs 60–90 minutes: warmup and meditation, guided instruction, partner drills and practice, and a group photo to close. A loaner saber is included with every RSVP — bring your own combat-ready saber if you have one.</p>
</div>

## Location

**Westchester Library Health and Wellness Information Center**

## Before you come

- Wear light, comfortable clothing you can move freely in.
- **Age:** no children under 13. Minors 13–17 must be accompanied by, and train only with, a guardian.
- A signed waiver is required to participate.
- By signing up you're confirming you're in good physical health to take part, and accepting that SaberCraft isn't liable for injury or health risk arising from participation. SaberCraft doesn't control or take responsibility for the venue itself beyond the space it rents or partners on.
- SaberCraft reserves the right to decline participation to anyone whose conduct is disruptive or disrespectful to the group. Attendees come from every age group and background — act accordingly.
- Photos and video from class may be used in SaberCraft's marketing, in print and online.

## Common questions

**How long is a class?**
1 to 1.5 hours, with a few short breaks throughout: meditation, instruction, drills and practice, then a group photo.

**Do I need my own saber?**
No — a loaner is included with your RSVP. See [what saber to buy or bring](what-saber-to-buy.md) if you want your own.

**Is this class for me?**
Classes are built for complete beginners through returning students alike. If you're unsure, [is this class for me?](is-this-class-for-me.md) walks through what to expect.
